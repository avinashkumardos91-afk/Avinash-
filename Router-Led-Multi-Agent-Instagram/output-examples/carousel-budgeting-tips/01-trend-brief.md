TREND BRIEF — budgeting tips for students
- Angle 1: Educational "framework/how-to" carousels are re-served by IG to people who didn't swipe — high save + share rates.
- Angle 2: DM shares from carousels are weighted 3–5x higher than likes — build slides people want to send to a friend.
- Angle 3: Carousels generate ~3x more engagement than single images; 8–12 slides is the sweet spot for educational posts.
- Hook styles that perform: slide-1 must be a hard hook (no second chance) — a number + promise ("5 money rules broke students swear by").
- Format note: lead with slide 1 as the hook; make every slide genuinely useful (tips, steps, frameworks) to earn the save.
- Urgency: EVERGREEN (student budgeting is a year-round evergreen; no expiring trend).
