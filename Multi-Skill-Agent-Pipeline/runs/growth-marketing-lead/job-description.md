# Growth Marketing Lead — Seed-stage D2C Skincare Brand (Remote, India)

## About the role
We sell skincare direct to consumers, so our growth *is* the business — every rupee of
ad spend and every repeat purchase shows up in this month's revenue. As our first Growth
Marketing Lead you'll own the full funnel from a cold ad impression to a loyal repeat
customer, and turn scrappy experiments into a repeatable growth engine.

## What you'll do
- Own **performance marketing** across Meta, Google and emerging channels — budget,
  targeting, creative testing, and ROAS.
- Build the **retention engine** — email/WhatsApp/SMS flows, subscriptions, and
  repeat-purchase campaigns that lift LTV.
- Run a disciplined **experiment cadence** — hypotheses, A/B tests, and a weekly
  readout of what's working and what to kill.
- Partner with creative on **ad and landing-page concepts** that convert, briefing and
  iterating fast.
- Own the **growth metrics** — CAC, ROAS, LTV, payback period — and report them cleanly
  to the founders.
- Set up **attribution and dashboards** so decisions are data-led, not vibes-led.

## Must-have
- 4+ years in **growth / performance marketing**, ideally D2C or e-commerce.
- Proven hands-on results scaling **paid social and search** profitably (real ROAS/CAC
  numbers you can speak to).
- Strong on **retention / lifecycle marketing** (email, WhatsApp, SMS, subscriptions).
- Fluent with **analytics and attribution** — GA4, Meta/Google Ads managers, a BI or
  spreadsheet model.
- A **test-and-learn operator** who ships experiments weekly and reads the data honestly.

## Nice-to-have
- Early-stage / 0→1 brand-building experience.
- Basic creative chops (can brief or mock a scroll-stopping ad).
- Familiarity with the Indian D2C ecosystem (Shopify, marketplaces, COD dynamics).
- Comfort with light SQL or no-code analytics.

## Level & scope
Senior / founding-marketer level. Works daily with the founders and the creative
partner; will hire a small growth pod as spend scales. **Success at 6 months:**
profitable paid acquisition at a defensible CAC, a live retention program measurably
lifting repeat rate, and a weekly experiment cadence the whole team trusts.

## How we hire
A focused 4-stage process — recruiter screen, a practical growth case, a
metrics/behavioural round, and a founder conversation. See the interview kit for the
exact competencies and questions.
