# Interview Kit — Growth Marketing Lead (Seed-stage D2C Skincare)

Derived from `job-description.md`. Competencies are numbered **C1…C6**; the scorecard
mirrors this numbering.

## Competencies & questions

### C1 · Performance marketing
- Walk me through a paid-social account you scaled. What were the starting and ending
  ROAS/CAC, and what actually moved them?
- A campaign's ROAS drops 30% week-over-week. What's your first hour of diagnosis?
- *A strong answer shows:* hands-on channel depth, speaks in real numbers, separates
  creative/targeting/bidding causes.

### C2 · Retention & lifecycle
- Design a lifecycle program for a skincare buyer from first purchase to subscription.
- Which one retention metric would you move first here, and how?
- *A strong answer shows:* thinks in LTV and repeat rate, knows email/WhatsApp/SMS
  flows, ties retention to margin.

### C3 · Experimentation & analytics
- Describe your weekly experiment cadence — how do you pick, run, and kill tests?
- How do you handle attribution when Meta, Google and GA4 all disagree?
- *A strong answer shows:* disciplined test-and-learn, honest about attribution limits,
  avoids vanity metrics.

### C4 · Metrics & financial rigour
- Build me a quick CAC/LTV/payback model out loud for a ₹599 product. What breaks it?
- At what payback period would you cut or scale a channel, and why?
- *A strong answer shows:* fluent unit economics, links spend to cash, defensible
  thresholds.

### C5 · Creative & collaboration
- How do you brief a creative partner for a scroll-stopping ad? Give a real example.
- Tell me about a disagreement with a founder on spend. How did it resolve?
- *A strong answer shows:* can turn insight into creative direction, collaborates and
  disagrees well.

### C6 · Founding / 0→1 growth mindset
- First 90 days, limited budget: what do you build first and what do you ignore?
- What's a scrappy growth experiment you ran with almost no resources?
- *A strong answer shows:* pragmatic prioritisation, bias to ship, comfort with
  ambiguity and small budgets.

## Interview stages
1. **Recruiter screen** (30 min) — motivation, logistics, C6 sanity check.
2. **Practical growth case** (90 min) — covers **C1, C3, C4**.
3. **Metrics + behavioural** (60 min) — covers **C2, C4, C5**.
4. **Founder conversation** (45 min) — covers **C5, C6** and mutual fit.

## Red flags
- Talks strategy but can't quote a real ROAS/CAC/LTV number they owned.
- Treats retention as an afterthought to acquisition.
- Chases vanity metrics (impressions, followers) over payback and repeat rate.
- Wants a big budget/team before proving a profitable channel.
