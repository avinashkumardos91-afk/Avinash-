# Founding Backend Engineer — Seed-stage Fintech (Remote, India)

## About the role
We move money for small businesses and every bug is someone's payroll, so correctness
and uptime are the job, not a nice-to-have. As our founding backend engineer you'll own
the core ledger and payments services end to end — from schema to on-call — and set the
engineering bar the next ten hires inherit.

## What you'll do
- Design and build the core **ledger, payments, and reconciliation** services in a
  typed backend (TypeScript/Node or Go).
- Model financial data for **correctness and auditability** — idempotency, double-entry,
  exactly-once processing.
- Integrate third-party **payment rails and KYC/AML providers**, handling retries,
  webhooks, and failure modes gracefully.
- Own **reliability**: instrumentation, alerting, and a humane on-call for money-movement
  paths.
- Write the tests, runbooks, and docs that let a small team ship fast without breaking
  trust.
- Partner directly with the founders on architecture trade-offs and what to build next.

## Must-have
- 4+ years building **production backend services** at meaningful scale or criticality.
- Strong with a **typed language** (TypeScript, Go, Java, or similar) and relational
  databases (PostgreSQL).
- Hands-on experience with **payments, ledgers, or another correctness-critical domain**.
- Fluency in **API design, idempotency, and distributed-system failure handling**.
- Comfort **owning services in production** — you've carried a pager and improved it.
- Clear written communication; you can explain a trade-off to a non-engineer.

## Nice-to-have
- Early-stage / 0→1 startup experience.
- Exposure to compliance (PCI, KYC/AML) or regulated environments.
- Event-driven architectures (Kafka, queues) and infra-as-code.
- Open-source contributions or a track record of mentoring.

## Level & scope
Senior / founding level. Works daily with the two founders and (soon) a small pod of
engineers. **Success at 6 months:** the ledger and one live payment rail are in
production with < 0.1% failed-transaction rate, alerting that catches issues before
customers do, and a documented on-call the next two hires can join without hand-holding.

## How we hire
A focused 4-stage process — recruiter screen, a practical technical exercise, a
system-design + behavioural round, and a founder conversation. See the interview kit for
the exact competencies and questions.
