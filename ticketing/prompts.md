### Prompt 1 — the assignment + brief

> Assignment 2 : Ticketing System (5th August)
> Build a ticketing system using Claude Code in VS Code.
> The problem
> Every organisation has the same broken loop. Someone has a problem — a laptop won't
> boot, a classroom projector is dead, a hostel tap is leaking, a customer found a bug.
> They tell someone. That someone forgets, or fixes it quietly, or is on leave. Nobody
> knows how many problems are open, who is handling what, or which ones have been
> waiting three weeks.
> A ticketing system replaces that loop with a record.
> A ticket is one reported problem. It is raised by someone, describes what is
> wrong, and lives through a lifecycle — raised, picked up, worked on, resolved,
> closed. Along the way it carries information: who raised it, when, how urgent it is,
> what kind of problem it is, who owns it now, and what has happened so far.
> Two kinds of people use it. The person who raises tickets wants to report a
> problem quickly and know it hasn't been forgotten. The person who resolves them wants
> to see everything waiting, decide what to do next, and not lose track.
> The system's job is to make sure nothing disappears, and that at any moment
> someone can answer: what is open, what is urgent, who owns it, and how long has it
> been sitting there. do it in 3d just like previous website but this is Ticketing System the brand name the website name
